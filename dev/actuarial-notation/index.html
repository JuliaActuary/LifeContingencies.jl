Actuarial notation

The descriptive API is exported by default. Short actuarial names are available through an explicit import or a qualified call, so that common variable names such as a and P remain available in your own code.

Commutation formulas

The arguments to these functions are policy durations. For issue age $x$, put $p(t)={}_tp_x$, $v(t)=\operatorname{discount}(lc.int,t)$, and $H=\lfloor\operatorname{omega}(lc)\rfloor$. The package uses a unit radix by default:

FunctionImplemented quantity
l(lc, t; basis=1.0)$\mathrm{basis}\,p(t)$
APV(lc, t)$p(t)v(t)$
D(lc, t)Alias of APV(lc, t)
C(lc, t)$v(t+1)[p(t)-p(t+1)]$
N(lc, s)$\sum_{t=s}^{H-1}D(lc,t)$
M(lc, s)$\sum_{t=s}^{H-1}C(lc,t)$

APV(lc,t) is the pure endowment ${}_tE_x={}_tp_x v(t)$: the value of one unit paid at t if the life survives. For joint lives, survival means the joint contingency remains in force. To value a complete insurance or annuity, use its constructor and present_value. Changing basis in l scales that call only; it does not change the life or other commutation functions.

using LifeContingencies: l, D, C, N, M

@assert D(lc, 1) == APV(lc, 1)
@assert N(lc, 0) ≈ present_value(AnnuityDue(lc))
@assert M(lc, 0) ≈ present_value(Insurance(lc))
[(t=t, lives=l(lc, t; basis=1_000), D=D(lc, t), C=C(lc, t)) for t in 0:2]

Conditional values from commutation ratios

On a complete annual mortality basis, with a nonzero D(lc, s), the classical ratios are

\[\frac{N(lc,s)}{D(lc,s)}=\ddot a_{x+s}, \qquad \frac{M(lc,s)}{D(lc,s)}=A_{x+s}.\]

The first is the life annuity due conditional on being alive at s; the second is the remaining annual death-benefit value. Both retain the original mortality selection and use interval discounting $v(s,t)=v(t)/v(s)$. With select mortality or a non-flat curve, creating a newly issued life at the attained age can change that basis.

s = 1
conditional_due = sum(survival(lc.life, s, t) * discount(lc, s, t) for t in s:(omega(lc)-1))
@assert N(lc, s) / D(lc, s) ≈ conditional_due
(conditional_annuity_due = N(lc, s) / D(lc, s), conditional_insurance = M(lc, s) / D(lc, s))
(conditional_annuity_due = 1.4761904761904763, conditional_insurance = 0.9297052154195011)

Lifetime sums require extinction by the last whole year covered by the mortality assumption. Incomplete tables, uncovered fractional terminal years and infinite support raise a DomainError. Use an explicitly termed product or endowment instead.
